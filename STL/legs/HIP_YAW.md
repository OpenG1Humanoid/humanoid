# Hip (thigh) yaw actuator

`*_hip_roll_link_integrated_yaw.stl` and `*_hip_yaw_link_knee_integrated_yaw.stl` replace the old hip roll and hip yaw links. They add the 6512 cycloidal actuator for the thigh yaw joint.

## Hip roll link (holds the actuator)
- A Ø70.5 × 32.5 mm pocket centred on the yaw axis (x = 25, y = 0 in the roll-link frame) opens downwards.
- The assembled actuator sits in it with the output facing down towards the thigh.
- The pocket floor has 12 M3 heat-insert holes on a 32 mm radius, every 30°, matching the 12 through-holes in the actuator housing. Put M3×35 bolts in from the output side, through the housing and into the inserts. At least 6 are needed.
- A Ø20 hole in the floor and a Ø9 channel out of the back of the link carry the motor wires.
- The bottom face is trimmed 0.4 mm so the two links don't rub.

## Hip yaw link (thigh)
- The top face has 6 M3 heat-insert holes on a 17.3 mm radius, every 60°, matching the large holes in the output-shaft front.
- A Ø13 recess under the output hub gives clearance.
- Fit the output shaft to the thigh before closing up the actuator, then finish building the actuator on top.

The links also had non-manifold edges in the old yaw-link file. Both have been rebuilt as clean, watertight meshes.
